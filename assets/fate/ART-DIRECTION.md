# FATE visual assets

The initial newborn and hand plates were created for this repository with the built-in Imagegen tool on 2026-10-31. Source PNG alpha channels are preserved. The later 3D witness uses the separately credited CC0 character base described below.

- `newborn.png` — 1536 × 1024 RGBA; sleeping newborn wrapped in ivory linen; current character silhouette source. Only its alpha channel is rendered: the character starts as solid black with emissive eyes and mouth, then becomes featureless white light. Skin, face and fabric RGB are never shown.
- `child.png` — 1024 × 1536 RGBA; earlier standing-child concept, retained for provenance and no longer used in the page.
- `hands-atlas.png` — 1254 × 1254 RGBA; four distinct sculptural hand poses in a 2 × 2 atlas, 0.77 MB. Only alpha is rendered; skin RGB is replaced with procedural deep-blue smoke.
- `congregation.png` — 1672 × 941 RGB; distant hand congregation, 1.73 MB. Blurred, thresholded luminance supplies a mass mask for dark blue mist, suppressing anatomical shading.

The scene uses image plates in a Three.js perspective camera, 72 or 192 instanced hand quads, procedural blade geometry, GLSL reveal/edge glow, particles and atmospheric light. The newborn and hands are not fully rigged 3D models; view angles are intentionally restrained to preserve the artwork. This is a deliberate depth-layered art direction, not a claim of 1,000 individually modeled hands.

The musical recording has separate provenance: it was provided locally by the site owner and is included unchanged in the repository and site build at their request. Playback is scoped to FATE. See the project README for autoplay behavior.

## Exact generation prompts

### Runtime silhouette treatment

The generated PNG remains unchanged. A smooth alpha threshold removes its original soft halo; the Three.js material renders a flat black silhouette, with three white apertures aligned to the newborn's pose. During score 71.5–79.5 (on the complete 0–377 scroll score) the fill becomes fully white and the eye/mouth apertures dissolve into it. A separate animated smoke pass samples the same contour, following the infant's breathing and rise. The still/reduced-motion version uses the same alpha silhouette through SVG filters and preserves both narrative states. No additional image generation was needed for this treatment.

### Runtime spectral hands

The original hand atlas remains unchanged. Its alpha channel defines the poses; no skin, veins or original lighting are sampled. Each instance is filled with slowly moving ink-blue noise and smoke strands. Nearby alpha samples soften the contour into vapor, while restrained edge light feeds the shared bloom pass. Lower forearms dissolve into the void, and slight vertex drift preserves the supporting fingertips. The distant congregation uses a blurred luminance threshold as its shape, recolored in dark blue smoke. The static/reduced-motion scene uses an SVG mask, solid blue fill and diffused rim, keeping the same direction without WebGL or animation. FATE retains its separate soundtrack and cold, oppressive atmosphere; these effects do not load on the Creation homepage.

### Katana and black meadow (procedural, 2026-11-01)

`fate/katana.js` builds a narrow curved shinogi blade with an angular kissaki, yokote, charcoal steel facets and fine crimson inlay. The user's supplied anime katana reference guides the open four-hook guard, red tsuka underwrap, raised black crossing bands, fittings and a draped chain made from individually modeled alternating links. The reference image itself is not shipped. The katana falls during scroll117–135; the monumental blade fills the frame before the extended reading passage. The enlarged blade reading pass occupies scroll135–219, leaving the right side open for factual notes from the main portfolio. The blade is scaled to twice its former size and settled eight world units below the earth, burying most of the kissaki. Terrain is fully present and opaque before it enters the frame. During the notes, an upward camera angle keeps the meadow and ground out of view; the subsequent camera descent reveals the already existing surface. A shared reversible `fate/score.js` map gives the reading passage84 scroll units instead of48 and the ground approach24 instead of12. After a separate downward reveal during219–243, the camera and target translate horizontally at fixed height during243–271. `fate/grove.js` now supplies a treeless meadow: dense individually curved grass ribbons and sparse bent seed heads over irregular black earth. Three merged meshes replace all tree, branch, shrub and fern geometry. A small open patch around the witness keeps the first shoe shot readable.

`reference-katana.svg` and `organic-grove.svg` are native vector projections of the procedural sword and meadow for static/reduced-motion mode. The katana has a fixed 45-degree yaw so its black/red face reads both during the earlier descent and from behind the boy.

### Current witness: black ink silhouette (2026-11-01)

The active route uses the approved `brave-boy-approved.png` on a fixed perspective plane, with the camera in `brave-camera.js` and geography in `brave-world.js`. Its path visits the planted katana, the carved footprint, the right sleeve, the left shoulder and the eyes. The earlier 3D orbit implementation described below is retained as history and is not loaded by this route.

`fate/brave-ink.js` derives a contour and the two blue iris masks from the unchanged 1024 by 1536 reference. A border-connected flood fill retains open background gaps and fills the light face and cloak lining. Signed distances drive a pure black body, animated black aura and flowing blue eye glow. The noise basis and slow advection match the spectral hands; a faint neutral reflection outside the body keeps the black contour legible. Original skin, clothing and background RGB never enter the rendered material. The padded plane has zero alpha at its borders and does not write depth through empty areas. No opaque replacement backdrop is used.

The actual clearing becomes darker during the return from the footprint; the former light ground patch used to disguise the RGB background has been removed. Character scale, position, camera path, katana and footprint geography remain fixed. Only the visible material animates in clock time. Rendering stops when the scene leaves the viewport or the page is hidden. Still mode, reduced motion and unavailable WebGL use updated direct scene renders in the seven `brave-*` frame pairs. The illustration remains flat, so it is intended for the existing restrained camera approach rather than an orbit or rear view.

### Earlier young witness (CC0 skinned 3D base, 2026-11-01)

`witness.glb` preserves the original CC0 Sakurada Fumiriya VRM/GLB bytes. The official author is pixiv Inc. / VRoid Project; [the author's CC0 statement](https://vroid.pixiv.help/hc/en-us/articles/360014788554-Sakurada-Fumiriya) and the distribution URL/checksum are retained in `witness-LICENSE.txt`. This is a derivative of an existing character base, not an exclusive original model and not the exact character in the user's reference.

`fate/witness.js` fits the source skeleton to young proportions and retains its face, neck and boots. The old shirt, tie, trousers and hair are excluded. A new volumetric wrap tunic, ivory inner collar, layered sash, wide pleated trousers, long lined cloak, hanging sleeves and raised hood follow the approved reference. Curved solid hair locks form the dark fringe and nape. The irises are blue; skin uses smooth, softly lit shading. The held pose is baked and merged into 19 material draws (about 47,000 triangles), and unused source geometry, materials and textures are released. The source file and its CC0 attribution remain unchanged. This is a stylized reference-led reconstruction using a licensed face base, not an exact scan or a claim of identical facial topology.

The approved sources are Library `libfile_6e4bdfa88b3c819186afa7b0ab6ae2e3`, version 1 (`fate-boy-03-older.png`, 1024×1536), and `libfile_117931e808c88191941b3073d295da49` (`fate-boy-approved-turnaround.png`, 1774×887). Both were downloaded and visually inspected locally before modeling. They remain development references in `.qa/approved-boy-references/` and are not shipped as animation frames.

The complete boy uses one uniform scale to reach 148 world units. The katana retains its original geometry, scale 2, eight-unit embed depth and information passage. The boy is about 1.4 times the full sword length. His soles remain on the ground at (112, 4); proportional camera distances and portrait framing prevent the size change from becoming a giant-child close-up. He stays hidden until the foot approach, so the enlarged model cannot intrude into the earlier sword notes. Camera far distance expands only during the witness approach; the earlier framing remains unchanged.

The meadow uses jittered planting cells instead of isolated tufts. There is no cleared circle around the witness. Two overlapping detail bands cover the original low meadow move and the wider orbit; a gradual transition avoids a rectangular density boundary when viewed from above. Earth, grass and seed heads remain three merged meshes. Desktop uses about 168,000 blades and mobile about 90,000, with fewer segments and wider spacing on mobile. `meadow-carpet.png` is a direct render of this geometry for static mode; `organic-grove.svg` is retained only as the earlier projection.

After internal cue234 the camera approaches from the boy's left. The shoe and garment hem fill the first shot at250. A genuine 270-degree orbit then rises through the back and the opposite side; the camera reaches measured eye height when it arrives in front at330. The back view pulls out to contain the full figure. The katana remains physically ahead of the boy in the shared scene and is visible beside him during the three-quarter back passage. The front shot holds to340; the return appears only from336. Movement is a pure reversible function of scroll. `witness-foot/back/face.png` and their `-mobile` variants are direct WebGL renders of this model at cues250, 287 and330 for static mode.
