# FATE visual assets

Created for this repository with the built-in Imagegen tool on 2026-10-31. These are original generated visual plates; no anime frames, existing character images or downloaded 3D models are used. Source PNG alpha channels are preserved.

- `newborn.png` — 1536 × 1024 RGBA; sleeping newborn wrapped in ivory linen; current character silhouette source. Only its alpha channel is rendered: the character starts as solid black with emissive eyes and mouth, then becomes featureless white light. Skin, face and fabric RGB are never shown.
- `child.png` — 1024 × 1536 RGBA; earlier standing-child concept, retained for provenance and no longer used in the page.
- `hands-atlas.png` — 1254 × 1254 RGBA; four distinct sculptural hand poses in a 2 × 2 atlas, 0.77 MB. Only alpha is rendered; skin RGB is replaced with procedural deep-blue smoke.
- `congregation.png` — 1672 × 941 RGB; distant hand congregation, 1.73 MB. Blurred, thresholded luminance supplies a mass mask for dark blue mist, suppressing anatomical shading.

The scene uses image plates in a Three.js perspective camera, 72 or 192 instanced hand quads, procedural blade geometry, GLSL reveal/edge glow, particles and atmospheric light. The figures are not fully rigged 3D models; view angles are intentionally restrained to preserve the artwork. This is a deliberate depth-layered art direction, not a claim of 1,000 individually modeled hands.

The musical recording has separate provenance: it was provided locally by the site owner and is included unchanged in the repository and site build at their request. Playback is scoped to FATE. See the project README for autoplay behavior.

## Exact generation prompts

### Runtime silhouette treatment

The generated PNG remains unchanged. A smooth alpha threshold removes its original soft halo; the Three.js material renders a flat black silhouette, with three white apertures aligned to the newborn's pose. During score 71.5–79.5 (on the complete 0–277 scroll score) the fill becomes fully white and the eye/mouth apertures dissolve into it. A separate animated smoke pass samples the same contour, following the infant's breathing and rise. The still/reduced-motion version uses the same alpha silhouette through SVG filters and preserves both narrative states. No additional image generation was needed for this treatment.

### Runtime spectral hands

The original hand atlas remains unchanged. Its alpha channel defines the poses; no skin, veins or original lighting are sampled. Each instance is filled with slowly moving ink-blue noise and smoke strands. Nearby alpha samples soften the contour into vapor, while restrained edge light feeds the shared bloom pass. Lower forearms dissolve into the void, and slight vertex drift preserves the supporting fingertips. The distant congregation uses a blurred luminance threshold as its shape, recolored in dark blue smoke. The static/reduced-motion scene uses an SVG mask, solid blue fill and diffused rim, keeping the same direction without WebGL or animation. FATE retains its separate soundtrack and cold, oppressive atmosphere; these effects do not load on the Creation homepage.

### Katana and black grove (procedural, 2026-11-01)

`fate/katana.js` builds a narrow curved shinogi blade with an angular kissaki, yokote, charcoal steel facets and fine crimson inlay. The user's supplied anime katana reference guides the open four-hook guard, red tsuka underwrap, raised black crossing bands, fittings and a draped chain made from individually modeled alternating links. The reference image itself is not shipped. The katana falls during scroll117–135; the monumental blade fills the frame before the extended reading passage. The enlarged blade reading pass occupies scroll135–219, leaving the right side open for factual notes from the main portfolio. The blade is scaled to twice its former size and settled eight world units below the earth, burying most of the kissaki. Terrain is fully present and opaque before it enters the frame. During the notes, an upward camera angle keeps the woodland and ground out of view; the subsequent camera descent reveals the already existing surface. A shared reversible `fate/score.js` map gives the reading passage84 scroll units instead of48 and the ground approach24 instead of12. After a separate downward reveal during219–243, the camera and target translate horizontally at fixed height during243–271. `fate/grove.js` supplies irregular tapering trunks, roots, curved recursive oak/birch limbs, individual folded leaves, grass ribbons, fern pinnae, rocks and fallen wood. Four merged terrain meshes keep the richer geometry bounded. The final clearing is static with a small return panel.

`reference-katana.svg` and `organic-grove.svg` are native vector projections of these same procedural meshes, preserving the revised forms in static/reduced-motion mode. No stock models, photographed trees or generated raster replacements are bundled. Woodland structure was studied from the [NPS oak woodland photographs](https://www.nps.gov/articles/wildland-fire-oak-and-savanna-midwestern-united-states.htm) and [the Bulgarian environment ministry's mature oak photographs](https://www.moew.government.bg/bg/novi-vekovni-durveta-sa-obyaveni-za-zastiteni-v-zemlisteto-na-ruse/); they are references, not downloaded site assets.
